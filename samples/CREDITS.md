# Sample photo credits

The sample photos are resized copies of photos from Wikimedia Commons, licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The file names
were changed to look like camera files. IMG_4014 is a blurred copy of IMG_4013,
and IMG_4025 is a darkened copy of IMG_4018. They were made to show how
Walkthrough handles soft and dark shots. These photos and their changed copies
are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
