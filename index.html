Walkthrough

Runs on this computer. Photos are never uploaded.

Sort a listing shoot in one pass.

Drop the photos from a property shoot. Walkthrough puts them in tour order, front of the house first. It sets aside dark, soft and repeated shots, and renames every file for the listing.

  1. Drop the shoot. JPEG, PNG or WebP. Up to a few hundred photos.
  2. Check the order. Change a room, move a photo, or put a shot back.
  3. Download the zip. Original files, renamed in order, with a shot list.

No photos to hand?

In the listing Drag to reorder, or use the arrows. The first photo is the cover.

    Set aside Not in the zip. Put a photo back if you want it.
